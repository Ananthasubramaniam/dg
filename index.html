Behind the Oven

Hi, I'm Subha Srihari.

It started in my kitchen in Pune, with one brownie recipe I couldn't stop making. I'd bake a batch, share it with friends, and watch it disappear in minutes. Soon, friends of friends started calling and asking if they could buy a box for their weekend dinners or birthdays.

That is how Dessert Groove was born. No venture capital, no massive industrial commercial kitchen. Just me, a couple of ovens, and a deep commitment to home-style baking.

I believe good desserts are made of simple, real things. I don't use commercial stabilizers, premixes, or artificial flavorings. If a recipe calls for butter, it gets pure, rich creamery butter. If it calls for chocolate, I use premium real chocolate. I still crack every egg and measure every gram of flour myself in small, controlled batches.

When you order from Dessert Groove, you're not getting a factory-assembled box from a retail franchise. You're getting a fresh dessert baked specifically for you by a friend who loves sweet things.
